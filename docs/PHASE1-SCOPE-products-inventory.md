# Voltix Phase 1 scope - Products, Inventory, Suppliers

## Working in Phase 1

- Browser-local product, supplier, and stock movement records.
- Product search, filtering, sorting, pagination, add/edit/archive/restore, and deletion controls.
- Inventory stock levels, low-stock alerts, adjustments, receiving deliveries, movement history, and CSV export.
- Supplier records, contact details, supplied-product view, activation/deactivation, and reference-safe deletion.
- Simulated sessions and role-aware UI permissions.
- POS-compatible stock mutation functions through `VoltixInventory`.

## Planned for later phases

- Real authentication and authorization.
- Backend database and multi-user synchronization.
- Barcode scanning.
- Warranty and serial-number tracking.
- Server-side audit logging, backups, and production deployment.
